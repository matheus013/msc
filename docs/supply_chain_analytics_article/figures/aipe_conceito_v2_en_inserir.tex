\caption{Conceptual overview of the Adaptive Inventory Policy Engine (AIPE). Heterogeneous histories are characterized at the store--item level. The Policy Selection Engine (PSE) either maps a given profile to a policy or predicts a policy from series features, optionally including the profile. Candidate policies are fitted on training data and selection uses validation evidence; cost, fill rate and selection stability are evaluated on held-out test data against global selection. Series, policies and mappings are illustrative; the figure neither presents experimental results nor assumes that contextual selection is superior.}
